# Prompts de las tomas IA — promo SysGym

Van en inglés porque los generadores responden mejor. Cada toma tiene dos partes:
- **Imagen:** el primer frame.
- **Movimiento:** para animar esa imagen con image-to-video, de 5 a 8 s.

**Bloque fijo:** pegalo al final de TODOS los prompts de imagen.

```
vertical 9:16, photorealistic, shot on iPhone 16 Pro, natural light, shallow depth of field, Argentine people, modern neighborhood gym in Buenos Aires. The smartphone screen is a flat, uniform, pure chroma green (#00FF00), fully visible, no reflections, no glare, no fingers over the screen, no UI, no text on screen. Phone held fairly upright and facing the camera.
```

**Negativo** (si la herramienta tiene campo para eso):

```
text, logos, watermark, UI on phone screen, reflections on screen, fingers covering screen, extra fingers, deformed hands, blurry phone, tilted phone, cartoon
```

---

## 00 · Hook
**Imagen:**
```
Tired gym owner in his 40s sitting at the reception desk at night, holding a smartphone, frustrated expression, rubbing his forehead, dim warm lights, empty gym behind him, a mate gourd on the desk.
```
**Movimiento:** `phone vibrates repeatedly, he sighs and looks at the screen, slow push-in`

## 01 · Aforo
**Imagen:**
```
Young woman in her 20s in activewear with a gym bag on her shoulder, standing at her apartment door, looking at her smartphone before leaving, morning light.
```
**Movimiento:** `she glances at the phone, small satisfied smile, subtle handheld camera`

## 02 · Pago de cuota
**Imagen:**
```
Man in his 30s at a gym front desk, holding his smartphone out toward the camera to show the screen, friendly receptionist blurred in the background.
```
**Movimiento:** `he raises the phone slightly toward camera and holds it still, slow push-in`

## 03 · Rutina que cuida el hombro
**Imagen:**
```
Young man sitting on a flat bench in the weights area, one hand on his right shoulder, the other holding a smartphone, looking at the screen thoughtfully, dumbbells around.
```
**Movimiento:** `he rolls his shoulder gently, then looks down at the phone and nods, handheld`

## 04 · Aviso de cierre
**Imagen:**
```
Close-up of a smartphone lying face up on a wooden kitchen table next to a coffee cup and keys, screen facing the camera, top-down 3/4 angle, morning light.
```
**Movimiento:** `the phone vibrates on the table, a hand enters the frame and picks it up`
*(Si el celular se mueve demasiado, usá solo la imagen quieta con un zoom lento en edición.)*

## 08 · Ranking y reto
**Imagen:**
```
Two women in their 20s in a gym, laughing and high-fiving after a workout, one holds a smartphone toward the other showing the screen, sweaty, energetic atmosphere.
```
**Movimiento:** `high five, then both look at the phone and laugh, slow orbit`

## 05 · Rutina para Marta
**Imagen:**
```
Fit male personal trainer in his 30s holding a smartphone, standing next to a smiling woman in her late 60s with short grey hair in activewear, in a bright gym, he shows her the phone screen.
```
**Movimiento:** `trainer taps the phone and turns it toward her, she nods and smiles, gentle push-in`

## 06 · Ingresos
**Imagen:**
```
Relaxed gym owner in his 40s sitting at the reception desk at night, holding a smartphone, calm satisfied expression, a mate gourd beside him, warm lamp light, closed gym behind.
```
**Movimiento:** `he scrolls with his thumb, takes a sip of mate, calm smile, very slow push-in`
*(Es el mismo personaje del hook, ahora tranquilo. Si la herramienta tiene referencia de personaje, usá la imagen del 00.)*

## 07 · Tema del gimnasio
**Imagen:**
```
Smartphone held in the foreground by a hand, in focus, in front of the entrance of a modern gym at dusk, dark storefront with neon green accent lights, blurred background.
```
**Movimiento:** `slow dolly forward toward the gym entrance while the phone stays centered`
*(Sin logo ni nombre en la fachada: el logo lo agregás en edición.)*

## 99 · Cierre
Sin prompt. Placa animada con el logo o la mascota en edición.
